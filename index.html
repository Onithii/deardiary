Autumn Archive · 01
The rain came softly

The afternoon turned silver with rain. We found the little path by the lake, pockets full of chestnuts, and watched clouds settle over the hills. I wish I could keep that hush here between these pages.

Wet leaves and woodsmoke filled the air. We watched a heron lift from the water's edge and vanish into the mist.

I tucked a copper leaf into my coat for the walk home. It rests here beside the date, a small promise to find this quiet again.

By the old bridge, rain made rings on the water. Our sleeves were damp, our pockets full, and we were both smiling all the way home.

14 October · at home
A little light to keep

Back home, the kettle sang as leaves tapped at the window. You read aloud, and I wrote down the line—though your voice was what I wanted to keep.

We let the tea grow cold, trading stories as lamplight gathered around us.

You told me about the pear tree, crooked gate, and wild mint in your childhood garden. I tried to remember every detail.

We promised to take the long way next time. By evening, the rain had softened and the whole room seemed to exhale.

A Letter

A warm sheet of laid paper unfolds downward, panel by panel—no envelope.

Folded Sheet · No Envelope
My dearest,

I have been thinking of that slow Sunday afternoon, when the rain softened the windows and we let the tea grow cold while we talked. It felt as though the world had given us a little extra time.

I remember the way you laughed at the story in the old book, and how the golden light moved slowly across the table while the kettle sang.

I keep returning to the small things: your coat by the door, the leaves pressed between the pages, and the quiet that settled around us when the rain began. They were ordinary moments, perhaps, but they have become some of my most treasured ones.

I hope the coming days bring you gentle mornings, a favorite song at just the right time, and somewhere peaceful to rest whenever you need it.

Until we meet again, know that you are remembered with such fondness. I am saving this little piece of our autumn here, so we can find our way back to it whenever we wish.

With all my love,
Always yours,
Mara

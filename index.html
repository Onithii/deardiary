The Philosophy

A quiet sanctuary for things worth keeping.

The modern internet asks you to perform—to broadcast every thought into an endless, noisy feed. DearDiary is the opposite: a calm, private library built around two tactile keepsakes—multi-page Books that turn like bound linen journals, and single-sheet Letters that unfold vertically in your hands.

No likes, no follower counts, and no envelopes to clutter the desk—just cotton rag paper, warm butter-yellow light, and your own words.

02 · Interactive Books

Scrapbooks with weight, texture, and turning pages.

Arrange photographs in Polaroid frames, fasten notes with serrated butter-yellow washi tape, and flip smoothly from spread to spread just like a cloth-bound keepsake on your coffee table.

03 · Folded Letters

Letters that unfold downward in your hands.

Some thoughts belong on a single sheet of paper rather than an entire book. DearDiary lets you write on tri-folded 120gsm cotton stationery that opens vertically panel-by-panel—never hidden inside an envelope.

No. 07 · Cotton Rag Sheet November 21, 2026

Tactile Bookbinding

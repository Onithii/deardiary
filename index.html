A Letter

A warm sheet of laid paper unfolds downward, panel by panel—no envelope.

Folded Sheet · No Envelope
My dearest,
I have been thinking of that slow Sunday afternoon, when the rain softened the windows and we let the tea grow cold while we talked. It felt like the world had given us a little extra time.
I keep returning to the small things: your laugh from the kitchen, the golden light on the table, and the book left open between us. They are ordinary moments, perhaps, but they have become some of my most treasured ones.
I hope the coming days bring you gentle mornings, a favorite song at just the right time, and somewhere quiet to rest when you need it. Until we meet again, know that you are remembered with such fondness.
With all my love,
Always yours
